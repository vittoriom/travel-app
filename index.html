Developer & Power User Ready

Native CLI & Built-in Model Context Protocol (MCP)

Control your trips from the terminal or connect Travel Companion to Claude Desktop, Cursor, and autonomous AI agents through the embedded MCP server.

Inspect upcoming itineraries, query nearby recommendations, and export trips directly from your zsh or bash terminal:

travel trip show "Japan Autumn" --day 3 --export gpx
travel concierge pivot --type rain --destination "Kyoto"

Add Travel Companion as an MCP server to Claude Desktop (`claude_desktop_config.json`) to let AI chat models query your trip plans directly: 

{
  "mcpServers": {
    "travel": {
      "command": "node",
      "args": ["/Applications/Travel Companion.app/Contents/Resources/mcp/server.js"]
    }
  }
}

Fast & Frictionless

Get Started in Seconds

Travel Companion is distributed as a lightweight, notarizable macOS universal application.

1

1. Download the DMG Archive

Grab the latest build for Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4) or Intel processors (~15 MB).

2

2. Drag to Applications Folder

Mount the DMG disk image and drag the Travel Companion icon into your /Applications directory.

3

3. Allow First Launch (macOS Gatekeeper)

As an independently compiled open-source binary, run this one-line terminal command if Gatekeeper prompts:

xattr -cr "/Applications/Travel Companion.app"
4

4. Add Your OpenRouter or AI API Key (Optional)

Bring your own key for OpenRouter, DeepSeek, Claude, or Gemini to unlock dynamic generation. Local templates work out-of-the-box offline without any key.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about privacy, offline support, AI keys, and FindPenguins.

Why not just use FindPenguins or Polarsteps directly?

−
FindPenguins and Polarsteps are outstanding tools for passive GPS tracking and printing beautiful memory photobooks after you return. However, they provide zero intelligence for the hardest parts of traveling: deciding pacing, allocating nights, discovering authentic local food, scheduling around locked bookings, or pivoting when it suddenly pours rain. Travel Companion is the intelligent brain before and during the trip, and delivers your itinerary cleanly into FindPenguins with zero manual data entry.

Do I need a continuous internet connection while traveling abroad?

+
No! Travel Companion is architected local-first. Once you generate or import your trip plan, all daily schedules, cultural POI pills, offline restaurant dossiers, and geocoded coordinates are cached locally in your browser/desktop storage. You can browse your complete itinerary in airplane mode or in remote mountain areas without international roaming.

What is a 'Hard Anchor' and why does it matter?

+
Most trips include 1 or 2 immovable events per day — such as a 13:00 museum entry, a booked train departure, or a Michelin-starred dinner reservation. When plans change, rigid apps break and generic LLMs forget the time constraint. In Travel Companion, you lock these as 'Hard Anchors'. All fluid POIs and coffee stops rearrange smoothly around your fixed commitments without ever displacing them.

Which AI models can I use, and does it cost money?

+
Travel Companion is 100% free, open-source software with Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK) architecture. You can connect OpenRouter to access DeepSeek V3/R1, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Google Gemini 2.0 Flash, or Llama 3. Pay pennies directly to the model provider for only the tokens you consume — no monthly $20 subscriptions or hidden markups.

How does the Model Context Protocol (MCP) integration work?

+
Travel Companion includes a built-in MCP server (`travel-mcp`). By configuring your `claude_desktop_config.json` or Cursor editor, external AI assistants can query your upcoming trips, search your food dossier, or check today's locked anchors as native tool calls directly in your conversation.
