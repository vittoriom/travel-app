Get Started in Seconds

Travel Companion is distributed as a lightweight macOS universal application.

1

1. Download the DMG Archive

Grab the latest build for Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4) or Intel processors (~15 MB).

2

2. Drag to Applications Folder

Mount the DMG disk image and drag the Travel Companion icon into your /Applications directory.

3

3. Allow First Launch (macOS Gatekeeper)

As an independently compiled open-source binary, run this one-line terminal command if Gatekeeper prompts:

xattr -cr "/Applications/Travel Companion.app"
Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about audioguides, privacy, offline support, AI keys, and FindPenguins.

How does the museum-grade audioguide generation work?

−
Every point of interest on your daily schedule can be enriched with a museum-grade audioguide. Travel Companion synthesizes a spoken storytelling script accompanied by historical background lore, key masterpiece highlights, and hidden architectural secrets. You can listen directly in the app with speech synthesis controls (pause, resume, 0.8x-1.25x speed) or read along with the text.

Why not just use FindPenguins or Polarsteps directly?

+
FindPenguins and Polarsteps are outstanding tools for passive GPS tracking and printing photobooks after you return. However, they provide zero intelligence for planning: deciding pacing, allocating nights, discovering authentic local food, scheduling around locked bookings, listening to audioguides, or pivoting when it suddenly pours rain. Travel Companion acts as your intelligent brain before and during the trip, and delivers your itinerary cleanly into FindPenguins with zero manual data entry.

Do I need a continuous internet connection while traveling abroad?

+
No! Travel Companion is architected local-first. Once you generate or import your trip plan, all daily schedules, audioguide narrations, cultural lore, restaurant dossiers, and geocoded coordinates are cached locally. You can browse your complete itinerary in airplane mode or in remote mountain areas without international roaming.

What is a 'Hard Anchor' and why does it matter?

+
Most trips include 1 or 2 immovable events per day — such as a 13:00 museum entry, a booked train departure, or a Michelin-starred dinner reservation. When plans change, rigid calendar apps break and generic LLMs forget the time constraint. In Travel Companion, you lock these as 'Hard Anchors'. All fluid POIs rearrange smoothly around your fixed commitments without ever displacing them.

Can external AI assistants (like Claude or Cursor) access my trips?

+
Yes! Travel Companion includes a built-in Model Context Protocol (MCP) server (`travel-mcp`) and a native CLI (`travel-cli`). By configuring Claude Desktop or Cursor, external AI agents can query your upcoming itineraries, inspect food dossiers, or check today's locked anchors directly via native tool calls.
